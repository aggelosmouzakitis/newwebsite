Somo Outdoors · Outdoor activity and community

Somo Outdoors

Small outdoor events for founders, solopreneurs, executives and independent professionals.

Each event is capped at 12 participants. You apply once and then book individual events through Luma.

Apply to Somo Outdoors→See the next event→

How it works

A simple model

  • Up to 12 participants at each event.
  • Approximately one event a month to begin with.
  • One short application to join the community.
  • Each event is booked through Luma.
  • Difficulty and required equipment are stated in advance.
  • Participants organise their own transport and preparation.

Activity and meeting people

Without formal networking

Somo Outdoors gives you the opportunity to meet people with similar professional experiences through the activity itself.

There are no pitches, presentations or organised exchanges of business cards. Persistent self-promotion and direct selling do not fit the community.

The application helps us keep the group small and consistent.

The events

Where we begin

Hike & Talk

A hike and a conversation

A hike with a clearly stated difficulty level, a short introduction and walking in changing pairs. It may include a quiet part of the route and optional coffee or food afterwards.

Walk & Talk

An easier walk

An easier walk through the city, a park or along the coast. Suitable for people who do not want a demanding hike.

Occasional events

Now and then

Depending on the season, we may organise outdoor movement, mindfulness, swimming or simple group activities.

Who it is for

How you take part matters more than your job title

Somo Outdoors is intended for:

  • Founders and solopreneurs.
  • Executives and managers.
  • Independent professionals.
  • Experienced specialists.
  • People building demanding careers or businesses.

Community culture

What we ask from members

01
Interest in other people
Come ready to meet people, not only to talk about your work.
02
Respect for boundaries
No one is pressured to share personal information.
03
No persistent pitching
The events are not used for direct selling or lead generation.
04
Reliability
Let us know in good time if you cannot attend after booking.

How to explain it to someone else

Somo Outdoors organises small outdoor events for founders and professionals. Groups are capped at 12 people, and people get to know each other through hiking, walking or another shared activity, without formal networking.

Application

Apply to Somo Outdoors

You apply once. If the community appears suitable, you will receive information about upcoming events and booking through Luma.

One link is enough.
Two or three sentences are enough.

We do not ask for sensitive information and you do not need to prove your professional status.

We have received your application

We will reply by email and, if the community appears suitable, send you the participation information.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why is there an application?

It helps us keep the group small and include people who understand the character of the events.

Do I need to be a founder?

No. Founders, executives and independent professionals from different backgrounds take part.

Is it a networking event?

Not in the usual sense. You will meet people, but the event is not organised around professional presentations or selling.

How many people attend?

Up to 12 people at each event.

How difficult are the activities?

The difficulty and requirements are listed on each Luma event.

Can I come alone?

Yes. The format helps participants get to know each other.

What happens if the weather changes?

The event may change or move to another date. You will be informed in advance.

What does the fee include?

The price and what it includes are listed on Luma. Transport, food and personal equipment are normally the participant's responsibility.

Can I invite someone?

You can send them the application link. Every new member completes the same short application.

How do cancellations work?

The booking and cancellation terms are listed on each Luma event.
