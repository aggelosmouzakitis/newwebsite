What SOMO is

Three different ways to take part

Each SOMO experience uses the body, movement and the group differently.

  • Somo Circles combines somatic shaking with conversation and reflection.
  • Somo Flow combines yoga with somatic shaking.
  • Somo Outdoors brings small groups of professionals together through outdoor activity.

How it works

What to expect from SOMO

01
Clear guidance
The facilitator explains what will happen, gives specific instructions and remains available throughout.
02
Your own pace
You can adapt an exercise, reduce the intensity, rest or stop.
03
Attention to the body
We begin with what you notice physically, without asking you to explain it immediately.
04
Small groups
The number of participants stays limited, allowing time and space for genuine interaction.

Somo Outdoors

A small professional community outside the office

Somo Outdoors organises hikes, walks and other outdoor events for people with demanding professional lives.

Each event is capped at 12 participants. People get to know each other through the activity and conversation, without formal networking or persistent pitching.

You apply once and then book individual events through Luma.
