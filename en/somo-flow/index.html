Somo Flow · Yoga and somatic shaking Coming soon

Somo Flow

A gentle yoga and movement class followed by somatic shaking. There is no personal sharing or group reflection.

Join the waiting list→What happens in a class →

What it is

Yoga, movement and somatic shaking

Somo Flow begins with gentle yoga and movement to prepare the body. The somatic shaking practice follows.

The focus stays on movement and physical awareness. You are not asked to discuss personal experiences.

Comparison

Somo Flow and Somo Circles

Two different experiences.

Somo Flow

  • Is mainly led through movement.
  • Includes yoga and somatic shaking.
  • Does not include group reflection.
  • Suits people looking for a physical practice.

Somo Circles

  • Begins with somatic shaking.
  • Includes conversation in a small group.
  • Provides space to explore the experience.
  • Suits people who want to look more closely at what they notice.

A class

What happens in Somo Flow

01
A quiet start
Time to notice your breathing and body.
02
Yoga and movement
Gentle movements with options for different levels of experience.
03
Somatic shaking
Simple positions that allow the shaking to appear gradually.
04
Rest
Time to notice how the body feels after the practice.
05
Closing
A gradual return to your usual pace.

Who it may suit

It may interest you if you are looking for

  • Gentle movement with adaptations.
  • Greater awareness of the body.
  • A way to try somatic shaking through yoga.
  • A physical experience without personal sharing.
  • A different type of group movement class.

How to explain it to someone else

Somo Flow is a gentle class combining yoga with somatic shaking. It is for people who want to move and try the physical practice without taking part in group reflection.
